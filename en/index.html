Why Smile Care

  • Honest pricing, written down

    Every treatment has a published price range. You get a written estimate before anything starts — no "we'll see after the X-ray".

  • Painless by design

    Modern local anaesthesia, rotary instruments and conscious sedation for anxious patients. You can stop the appointment at any point.

  • Booked, not queued

    Request a slot online or on WhatsApp and get a confirmation within 30 minutes during clinic hours. Your chair is reserved.

  • We follow up

    A 12-month review is included after major treatment, and you keep your dentist's number for emergencies.
