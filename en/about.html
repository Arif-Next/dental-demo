About the clinic

Smile Care Dental opened in 2012 with one chair, one dentist and a waiting room that was really the corridor. Fourteen years later we have four chairs, a digital X-ray room and a team of six — and the same rule: explain first, treat second.

Our story

The clinic started in Dhanmondi in 2012 with a single chair and a single dentist.

The practice changed when we bought our first digital X-ray. For the first time we could show patients the problem on a screen instead of describing it — and the conversation in the chair changed with it.

The clinic

Sterilisation

  1. Used instruments leave the room in a sealed tray
  2. Ultrasonic cleaning
  3. Pouched and autoclaved at 134°C (Class B)
  4. Logged with date and cycle number, stored sealed

Ask to see the log — we keep it at reception.
